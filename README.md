# Resume Match Score

I want to build ATS resume score predictor based on the job description of a role and the resume that I am attaching. The job description could be just a copy-paste. Text from a website or an attachment, and the resume will be a document attachment, PDF or a Word doc or something of that sort. Go through how usually ATS resume predictors build and do a thorough research around how companies will be doing. ATS predictions, and I want to get a thorough score around whether this particular resume is strong enough to be submitted for a particular role and job description. And if not, what- ... particularly should be improved without changing the meaning and the work done in the resume a lot. All right.
